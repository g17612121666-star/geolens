# Map data

- Plate boundaries and polygons: Peter Bird (2003), An updated digital model of plate boundaries, DOI 10.1029/2001GC000252. GeoJSON conversion by Hugo Ahlenius / Nordpil and contributors: https://github.com/fraxen/tectonicplates. Open Data Commons Attribution 1.0: https://opendatacommons.org/licenses/by/1.0/. Short PB2002 steps retain boundary type and model relative speed. Longitudes are unwrapped per segment; polygon geometry is retained.
- Country land boundaries: Natural Earth 1:110m, public domain: https://www.naturalearthdata.com/about/terms-of-use/. Source https://github.com/nvkelso/natural-earth-vector/blob/master/geojson/ne_110m_admin_0_boundary_lines_land.geojson . Generalised and not authoritative for territorial claims.
- Historical earthquakes, climate example points, currents, rivers, basins and movement arrows: Geolens teaching selections. These are explicitly labelled examples or schematic paths, not complete spatial datasets or live feeds.
