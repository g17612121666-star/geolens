# Botanical and ground materials

The following 2048 × 2048 PBR maps were downloaded on 20 September 2026 from Poly Haven and encoded as WebP at their original resolution. These are photographed material surfaces, used on constructed 3D teaching models. They are not field photographs of the scene.

| Files | Original | Author | Licence |
|---|---|---|---|
| `bark_brown_02_{diff,nor_gl,rough}.webp` | https://polyhaven.com/a/bark_brown_02 | Rob Tuytel | CC0 |
| `forest_ground_04_{diff,nor_gl,rough}.webp` | https://polyhaven.com/a/forest_ground_04 | Rob Tuytel (photography/processing), Rico Cilliers (adjustment) | CC0 |

Original download pattern: `https://dl.polyhaven.org/file/ph-assets/Textures/jpg/2k/{asset}/{asset}_{map}_2k.jpg`.
Diffuse maps use sRGB; normal and roughness maps use linear data. Bark scale is illustrative on trees whose size may be enlarged for teaching.
Leaf and needle meshes are original procedural geometry. Water ripples are procedural optical normals tied to the lesson clock; they do not represent measured wave height or velocity.

## Coastal rock

rock_face_{diff,disp,rough}.webp: Greg Zaal (photography), Dario Barresi (processing), Poly Haven, CC0. Source: https://polyhaven.com/a/rock_face . Original 2048 x 2048 maps, encoded as WebP quality 90; 1,005,368 bytes in total. Publisher MD5 values verified before conversion; source URLs, hashes and output SHA-256 values are in rock_face.source.json. Used on an illustrative headland, not a reconstruction of a named coast. Three-direction mapping prevents stretching around the cave and arch. The photographed patch spans 2.4 m; the model uses a common 10 m scene scale.
