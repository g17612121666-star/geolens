## Scientific and rendering limits

These remain idealised educational models with explicit scales, not scans of named field sites. Surface materials and detailed vegetation improve the world representation without pretending that invented topography is surveyed terrain. Vegetation, grains, roots and process tracers are enlarged when needed and are disclosed in scale text or the relevant inspection note. Snow coverage is a teaching representation, not a solved snow-depth field. The coastal, fluvial and aeolian landform stages are selected geometries; their controls do not imply uncalculated formation ages or transport rates.

Five models are intentionally static: contours, landform-types, soil-catena, soil-profile and soil-texture. Their meaningful controls alter geometry/readings. An unrelated continuously turning object would misrepresent their science, so none was added.

Formula-specific spans were added for the karst/weathering carbonate reaction, the groundwater expression, the soil-fraction sum and the water-budget equality. Shared `ScientificText` and the font rule are owned by the integrating task. `water-cycle` reuses the atmosphere laboratory’s `CloudVolume` without modifying its file.
